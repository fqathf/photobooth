# Photobooth Event Landing Page Design

## Overview
The website's visual identity should mimic the provided reference poster (`ref-1.jpeg`), creating a retro/vintage newspaper or magazine aesthetic.

## Background
- Use `bg.webp` (crumpled paper texture) as the global background for the webpage.

## Typography
- **Headings**: Use a bold, condensed, sans-serif or slab font (e.g., Impact, Anton, or similar) for major headings like "GABRIELA ABIGAIL'S 20TH BIRTHDAY" and "SAVE THE DATE & MAKE MEMORIES WITH ELLA!". Font color is solid black.
- **Subheadings & Labels**: Use a bold serif font (e.g., Playfair Display or Times New Roman) for labels like "Event by:", "Event Periode:", "Limited Freebies:".
- **Body Text**: Use a standard readable serif font for the bottom quote.

## Layout Structure (Desktop: Split Column, Mobile: Stacked)

### 1. Header Section (Top Center)
- "PHOTOBOOTH EVENT" (Serif, all caps)
- "GABRIELA ABIGAIL'S 20TH BIRTHDAY" (Huge, bold, condensed, black)
- "IN COLLABORATION WITH [Logo]"

### 2. Main Content (Two Columns)
**Left Column (Visuals):**
- Two photostrips displayed side-by-side (or just one prominent one for the studio).
- The photostrip design features a black background with white polka dots and a "stamp-like" jagged border.
- Floating "Arc 20" badges pinned to the photostrip.

**Right Column (Information):**
- **Event by:** @gabycoolyeah x @Nokizarupage
- **Event Periode:** 1 Agustus - 22 Agustus 2026
- **Limited Freebies:** Pin Bottle Cap
- **Visuals:** Two bottle caps containing photos of the birthday girl, with a "20" watermark overlay.
- **Terms:** 
  - *Exclusive at Blok M Square only

  - 📸📸 2x Photos -> Get 2 Pins
- **Call to Action Text:** "SAVE THE DATE & MAKE MEMORIES WITH ELLA!" (Very large, condensed black text).

### 3. Footer Section
- A distinct boxed area with a top/bottom border line.
- Contains the quote: "In a world that often makes us our own toughest critics, I hope you never forget to be kind to yourself. Cherish every lesson, celebrate every milestone, and trust that every step you've taken has led you exactly where you're meant to be." (Serif font).
